<div class="defender-container">
	<div class="defender-label">FortiEDR - Simulation Mode Detection</div>

**Login** into the **FortiEDR** console (FortiCloud) like before and navigate to `Incidents`.

Locate the incident on the domain controller for the file `danger_malware.exe` (extracted from `gmtmX.zip`). Notice that FortiEDR **detected and classified** the file as **Malicious**, but the ransomware still ran to completion and encrypted the files on the Engineeringadmin desktop.

This is because the **Ransomware Prevention** policy applied to this collector is currently set to **Simulation** mode — FortiEDR only alerts on the malicious behavior, it does not block it.

<img src="/images/EDR-ransomware-incident.jpg" alt="FortiEDR Ransomware Incident (Simulation)" style="max-width: 100%; height: auto;">

Click the incident to expand it and open `Investigate`. Review the `Activity Audit` panel — you'll see the classification change to **Malicious**, but no remediation/response action (such as **process terminated** or **beacon destroyed**) was taken, because the policy only observed the activity.

<img src="/images/EDR-ransomware-investigate.jpg" alt="FortiEDR Ransomware Investigate (Simulation)" style="max-width: 100%; height: auto;">

</div>

<div class="defender-container">
<div class="defender-label">FortiEDR - Switch to Prevention Mode</div>

To stop this ransomware from succeeding in the future, go to `Communication Control` (or `Security Profiles` / `Policies`, depending on your console layout) and locate the **Ransomware Prevention** policy assigned to the domain controller's collector group.

Switch the policy's enforcement mode from **Simulation** to **Prevention**. This tells FortiEDR to actively **block and remediate** matching malicious behavior instead of only logging it. **Wait for the instructor to confirm this change.**

<img src="/images/EDR-ransomware-policy.jpg" alt="FortiEDR Ransomware Prevention Policy" style="max-width: 100%; height: auto;">

</div>

Go back to `Incidents` in FortiEDR and open the **new incident** generated by this second execution attempt.

In the `Activity Audit` panel, notice the difference compared to the first run: FortiEDR now shows an active **response action** — the malicious process was terminated (and, depending on configuration, related artifacts remediated) **before** the files could be encrypted.

Check the `Violated Policies` panel to confirm **Ransomware Prevention** blocked the attempt, and review `Devices involved` / `Processes involved` to confirm the domain controller was the only host impacted.
